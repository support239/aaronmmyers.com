A quick note: This is a simple, original quiz to help you think about your work style. It is not the official Wealth Dynamics test. It does not measure your skills or promise business success.

How to take the quiz

Read each question. Pick the answer that sounds most like you. There are no right or wrong answers. Choose what you would really do, not what you think you should do.

  • There are 16 questions.
  • It should take about 5–8 minutes.
  • At the end, you will see your top two business styles, strengths, weak spots, and ideas for what to do next.
